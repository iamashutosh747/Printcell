    How the status is decided
    1. Green: total dues are Rs 2,500 or less.
    2. Black: cheques past their date, and bills past credit + 30 days exceed total PDC.
    3. Gray: cheques past their date.
    4. Blue: bills past credit + 30 days exceed total PDC.
    5. Red: bills past credit days exceed total PDC.
    6. Yellow: PDC covers bills past credit days, but the oldest bill (LBP) is past credit days.
    7. Green: none of the above.

    Rules are checked in this order; the first one that applies sets the status.
